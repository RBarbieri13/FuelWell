FuelWell helps you decide what to do next with food, activity, and recovery.

Log meals quickly, review your macro direction, and use coach-style guidance to keep the day realistic. The app is built for people who want practical nutrition decisions without turning every meal into spreadsheet work.

FuelWell can help you:

- Understand whether today is on track
- Log meals and photos
- Review macro adherence
- Plan groceries and recipes
- Get restaurant guidance
- Connect activity and recovery context
- Review and manage account data from Settings

FuelWell is focused on clarity, habit-building, and practical next steps.
