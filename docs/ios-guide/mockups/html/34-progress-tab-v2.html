Mockup notes · M34 Progress v2

Range selector
4-segment pill: Weekly (default) · Monthly · 90-day · All. All cards re-scale to the active window. Variation 2 changes the default to Monthly so reviewers see how the heavier charts behave.
Health Score trend (NEW)
First card. Top eyebrow HEALTH SCORE, big DM Sans 700 score (86.4), 30-day delta pill (+4.2), 56pt sparkline beside. Tap → M26 Health Score detail. The composite is the single number that integrates everything below it — putting it first lets the user see "am I trending in the right direction?" before diving into pillars.
Weight
Line chart of weekly averages (V1 single-line, V2 line + 7-day moving-average overlay, V3 line + dot-per-entry). Below the chart: a summary stat ("178.1 lb · −2.4 lb this month") and a primary "+ Add weight" button.
Macro adherence
Seven daily stacked P/C/F bars across the week. Adherence is shown as percentage of target hit per day — bars stack 0–100. Today highlighted (Variation 1 with a white dot on the topmost stack, Variation 2 with a colored ring); future days dim to bg.border. Summary stat: "Hit your macros 5 of 7 days" or "On pace · 71% of week".
Body photos
Horizontal scroll of 96×128 thumbs. Date overlay along the bottom edge. Trailing tile is a dashed add-photo card. Photos render as cool-tone gradient placeholders per the no-hand-drawn-imagery rule.
Measurements
Standard iOS-pattern list of waist / hips / chest / arms / etc., trailing trend pill colored green (down, in plan) / amber (up, watching) / neutral grey (flat). Tap row → full history sheet.
Mood & energy
V1 Weekly dots — 7 circles with emoji-ish glyphs in tinted backgrounds. V2 Weekly dots + a 5-row × 7-col heatmap of the last 5 weeks. V3 Weekly dots only, more compact.
Habit Tracking (NEW)
Compact 14-day × 4-habit dot grid in color.semantic.premium purple. Each row shows the habit name + count, then a 14-day dot grid (on / miss / future), trailing % completion. "View all habits" link beneath routes to M27 Habit Tracking detail. Moved off Dashboard — accessed from here and from the Menu only.
Empty state
Cards collapse to an inline coach-voice line. Top card swaps to a "Building your baseline" panel matching the Dashboard's day-1 treatment. Coach copy: Progress fills in as you log. Weight, photos, mood — one entry kicks it off. Single primary CTA pinned at the top.
