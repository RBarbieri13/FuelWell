Mockup notes · Hero redo (round 3)

V1 Grain bowl
Overhead shot, warm linen surface, white ceramic bowl over a quinoa base. Visible ingredients: kale leaves (four), cherry tomato halves (three), avocado wedges (two with score marks), chickpeas (five), sesame seeds and chopped herb garnish. Wooden spoon edge bottom-right. Top-light highlight on the rim.
V2 Garden salad
Overhead shot, white marble with subtle veining, white ceramic plate. Visible ingredients: romaine + dark leaves (six), cucumber slices with seed pattern (three), cherry tomato halves (three), red onion rings (two), lemon wedge with rind segments, microgreens, olive-oil drizzle highlights.
V3 Buddha bowl
Overhead shot, walnut wood board with grain and knots, white ceramic bowl, quinoa base. Sectioned ingredients: salmon fillet (top, with flesh lines + dill), roasted sweet potato cubes (right), broccoli florets (bottom-right, three clusters with stems), kale (bottom-left), avocado wedges (left, with score marks), lemon wedge corner, sesame seeds, microgreens.
Lighting
Top-light highlight on every bowl rim, soft drop shadow beneath, vignette overlay (22–34% alpha) at the edges. Reads as editorial photography rather than flat illustration.
Wordmark + CTA
Identical across all three. Outfit 700 36pt at -0.025em; gradient.brand CTA at 48pt with elevation.cta halo; Inter 500 secondary link. Brand-green appears only in the gradient.
