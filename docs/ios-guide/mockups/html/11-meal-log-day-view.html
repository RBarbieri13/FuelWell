Mockup notes · Step 4

Day strip
Seven 14pt-radius pills on white, today's date stays inverted with the brand-green pulse dot. Tap to switch day; swipe past for older. Past days carry verdict-color dots; future days are flat.
Summary band
The DESIGN.md inverted-row carried up from the Dashboard. Calorie total + remaining wrapped around a 56pt ring (progress in green, error-red on over). Macro fingerprint beneath in a divided row — same swatches as M09 & M10. On past days the ring fills to 100%; on future days it shows the planned total.
Section headers
Outfit 600 15pt with a 4pt color pip per meal type — carbs-amber for breakfast, protein-green for lunch, info-blue for snacks, fat-purple for dinner. Right side shows the section total (or "pending", italicized) in DM Sans tabular.
Meal rows
48pt thumbnail tile (gradient placeholder per food type), name in Inter 600, meta line with time + source tag (Recipe / Rest. / Pantry / Plan) + macro fingerprint. Trailing kcal in DM Sans 600 16pt tabular. Tap row → 3.13 Food Detail; long-press → quick-edit popover.
Pending rows
Dashed border, transparent fill, no shadow. Name reads "Add <mealtype>"; meta shows the coach's projection ("Coach suggestion ready") or the planned meal name when from Meal Plan. Tapping opens 3.12 Add Meal sheet.
Closed-day strap · V2
Once the day closes (midnight rollover), the dark summary band stays but a green confirmation strap sits beneath the day strip: "Closed at 1,932 kcal — within plan." Quietly informational, not celebratory.
Plan callout · V3
Future days where the Plan Generator has filled meals get a single dark plan-strap above the sections: "From your plan · Generated Wed · Edit". Each meal row carries the purple Plan source tag to make the provenance obvious.
