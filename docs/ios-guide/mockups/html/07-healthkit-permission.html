Mockup notes · Step 4

Progress dots
Seven of nine filled. Nav row matches M03–M06.
Hero
92pt rounded square (radius.xl 26pt) on white with a 1pt hairline and elevation.2. Holds Apple Health's red heart at full saturation — that color is fixed by convention even inside our light system (see color.semantic.error note in DESIGN.md). A 32pt badge in the corner carries the linking glyph (V1 / V2) or the brand-green check (V3) so the hero doubles as the success affordance.
Title block
"Connect Apple Health" in Outfit 700 26pt, centered. Subtitle: "We read four metrics to keep your plan accurate — never written back, never leaves your device." The "read" bold pulls the contractual word forward in the otherwise muted body color.
Metric rows
Single white card, radius.lg, with four rows separated by color.bg.borderSoft hairlines. Each row carries a 34pt tinted icon square, the metric name in Inter 600 15pt, a one-line why in color.text.secondary 12.5pt, and a "READ" pill on the right. The pill flips to a green "CONNECTED" affirmation + the last-synced value once HealthKit returns data (V3) — the row reads as both contract and live data source.
Trust strip
Below the metric card: a single-line claim with a lock glyph — "Read-only. Stays on your device. No third parties." The lock is rendered in color.text.primary on a 6% neutral wash so it doesn't compete with the hero color for attention.
Primary CTA · always enabled
Permission screens never disable Continue — the user must always be able to advance. V1 reads "Connect Apple Health" with the heart icon to telegraph the next sheet; V3 flips to "Continue" once permission has resolved. Skip-for-now sits beneath the CTA in color.text.secondary and advances to 3.8 with all metrics manual.
iOS HealthKit sheet · V2
Native-system mock: rounded-top tray on a scrim, FuelWell app glyph at the top, "FuelWell Would Like To Access Your Health Data" in SF Pro 17 bold, four data categories listed below an "Allow 'FuelWell' to read data" label, each with the iOS green switch in the on position. Bottom carries "Don't Allow" / "Allow" buttons — matches Apple HIG exactly. Status-bar time stays at 9:41 as our content underneath dims into the scrim.
Success · V3
Hero gains a green halo + a gradient.brand checkmark badge. Each metric row now shows its real value: 178.4 lb, 8,432 steps, 3 sessions, 412 kcal in DM Sans 600 tabular — first real numbers the user sees in the app. CTA reads "Continue" — flow advances to 3.8 Notifications.
