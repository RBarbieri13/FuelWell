Mockup notes · Step 4

Hero
4:3 photo placeholder with the four-ingredient blob pattern (chicken, sweet potato, broccoli, balsamic). Glassy nav row floats top — back, share, heart, ⋯.
Title block + meta
Recipe title in Outfit 700 22pt with -0.018em. Meta row: 25 min · 4 servings · 540 kcal/serving in color.text.secondary with each numeric value bumped to color.text.primary DM Sans tabular.
Fit strap
Dark band under the meta: 44pt brand-green progress ring + "94% fit for tonight — lands your protein gap and 32 kcal under budget." Tap → expanded breakdown that mirrors M15's pick-detail card. Replaces the wireframe's plain meta line with engine context.
4-cell macros
Kcal · Protein · Carbs · Fat each in a small white card. DM Sans 700 tabular. These render per serving; the serving stepper in the next section is what scales them in the log.
Actions
Gradient-brand "Log this meal" primary + three ghost square buttons (Add to grocery, Add to plan, ⋯ more). Long-press primary → portion-editor sheet (3.13) with quantity pre-bound.
Ingredients
Right-aligned servings stepper inside the section header. List shows checkbox + qty + name with a tiny "Have it" pantry pill in success-green for items already in your pantry. Tap a row → strikethrough + checkbox fills with inverted-dark (the "I have this" state).
Steps
Each step is a card with a 32pt dark numbered tile. Time-bound steps carry a blue info pill ("28 min"). Tapping a card promotes it into Cooking mode (V3).
Sticky compact nav · V2
Once the user scrolls past the hero, a frosted-glass bar slides in with back, the recipe name in Outfit 700 15pt (1-line clamp), and the heart. The hero photo scrolls away underneath; the page reads as a long-scroll recipe.
Cooking mode · V3
Dark immersive overlay launched from "Start cooking" or any step tap. Top: step progress bars (one per step, current segmented). Body: STEP 3 / 6 eyebrow, the full instruction headline in Outfit 700 26pt + a supporting detail paragraph. Below: a live 28-min timer card (ring shows remaining), then the ingredients-for-this-step mini-card. Bottom: prev / next nav with the gradient-brand "Next step →" primary.
