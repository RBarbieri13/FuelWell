Mockup notes · M30 Sankey full-screen

Band geometry
Bands are cubic-Bezier ribbons connecting a source rect on one edge to a destination rect on the canonical intake/expenditure columns. Thickness across the band equals the source kcal value scaled into the available axis range; bands ease in and out via control points at 50% of the horizontal (or vertical) distance.
Color
Intake bands are colored by their dominant macro — protein-heavy meals in #00D278, carb-heavy in #F59E0B, fat-heavy in #A855F7. Pending/projected meals (dinner before logging) render at 35% opacity with a dashed band-edge as the visual cue. Expenditure bands follow the source palette: BMR #6B7280, activity #00B4D8, workout #E87A1D.
Center columns
Two adjacent inverted (color.bg.elevated) pillars labeled TOTAL INTAKE and TOTAL OUT, each marked with its kcal in DM Sans 700. The gap between them (where they don't quite line up vertically) is the visualization of net surplus/deficit; a small "Net +128" tag bridges them with the semantic color of the verdict.
Layouts
V1 Classic reads left → right; the most natural translation of the metaphor and the spec primary. V2 Vertical flips to top → bottom which scans better on a narrow phone column (intake "fills" the day, expenditure drains it). V3 Hybrid places the three summary stats above the Sankey and the lane detail directly below in one continuous reading column — most scannable.
Detail rows
Tap-target rows beneath the Sankey for every lane. Intake rows carry P/C/F macro chips; expenditure rows carry source labels and methods (Apple Health vs FuelWell-logged). Pending lanes render dashed with the projected kcal in muted text. Tap → drill-down sheet (not in this mockup; lives at the route /energy/lane/:id).
Empty state
Replaces the Sankey card with a single dashed empty-state panel: Log meals and workouts to see your energy flow. Today's view fills in as you go. Summary tiles still render as zeros; detail-row section collapses to a single "+ Log a meal" CTA.
