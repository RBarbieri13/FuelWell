Mockup notes · Step 4

The magic moment
Per Max's review: payoff, not confirmation. No celebratory chrome — no confetti, no streak, no haptic.success burst. The reveal earns weight from the numbers + a single gradient.text headline phrase. DESIGN.md anti-pattern: "FuelWell rewards with information, not vibration."
Progress dots
Nine of nine filled. All steps done. No back chevron — the plan is committed and edits live in 3.23 Your Plan / Profile.
Title block
Eyebrow "PLAN READY" with a small success-green pulse dot, then Outfit 700 30pt headline "Your plan, live now." — the second clause picks up gradient.text (#00D278 → #00B4D8) per DESIGN.md, the only place gradient-text appears in onboarding. Subtitle below: "Calibrated to your inputs. We'll recalibrate as your body and life change."
Hero grid
Single bordered hero card, radius.xl 28pt, with a 1.5pt color.semantic.success stroke and a soft green-tinted drop shadow — the only "celebratory" affordance. 2×2 grid: Calories top-left (kcal slightly larger at 48pt), Protein top-right, Carbs bottom-left, Fat bottom-right. Each cell carries a 10pt swatch matching the macro token (color.macro.protein/carbs/fat) introducing the color vocabulary the Dashboard will reuse. Numbers in DM Sans 600 42pt tabular figures, unit caplet underneath.
Numbers
Computed for 32-yr-old female, 158 lb, moderate activity, maintain (the inputs we showed in M03 & M04): 2,260 kcal · 160 g protein · 245 g carbs · 70 g fat. Round numbers, no hedging — the engine commits.
Why-this-plan expander
Critical per Max — never cut. Single card below the grid with an info-circle glyph in info-blue, "Why this plan" label, chev right. Tapping reveals a four-sentence rationale + a row of assumption chips (Maintain · Moderate · 158 lb · 32 yr · Female · Light dietary constraints). Tapping a chip in the real app drills into the editor; here they're surfaced as the data foundation behind the numbers.
Primary CTA
"Start tracking" — 52pt tall (taller than other onboarding CTAs to telegraph this is the terminal step), gradient.brand + elevation.cta halo, right-arrow glyph trailing. V1 (computing) shows the disabled-dim variant since the plan isn't ready yet.
Computing state · V1
Hero grid renders as four shimmer skeletons while the engine resolves. A single "Computing your plan…" strap sits below with a green spinner and the running subtitle "Resolving 5 inputs · 2 constraints · activity level". 1.2s typical resolution time in spec; visible loader to telegraph the work being done.
Hooks · Dashboard 3.10
The macro swatches and number format introduced here are the same vocabulary the dashboard ring + macro rows will use — establishes the visual contract on the first surface, so the dashboard reads as continuity rather than a fresh layout.
