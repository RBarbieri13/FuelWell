Mockup notes · M31 redesigned

Top · Today's workout
Inverted bg.elevated card with the workout name (e.g. "Upper push"), duration in font.numeric 700, and a status badge — Completed in semantic.success tint or In progress in semantic.info tint. Dumbbell glyph in the orange tile on the left.
Stat tiles · active minutes / steps / burned
Three white cards in a 2×2 grid (calories burned spans the full bottom row). Big DM Sans 800 numbers at 32pt against text.primary. Each tile carries a small inline chart: active minutes uses a 7-day sparkline (cyan), steps uses a 7-day mini bar chart (premium purple), burned uses an orange→deep-orange gradient. Delta chip ties to the 7-day average.
Hero rotation chart
Bottom card. 4-tab pill segmented control: Day · Week · Month · Year. Default state is Week: 7 bars of daily calories burned across Mon–Sun, today rendered in green to flag the focus. A dashed average line overlays the chart with a small "AVG" pill on the right. Bars use an orange gradient fill to give the chart a vibrant cast against the light surface.
