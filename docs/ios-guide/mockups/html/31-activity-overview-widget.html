Mockup notes · M31 widget studies

Eyebrow & route
ACTIVITY · TODAY in body 700 caption at text.secondary, with an orange figure-run iconlet at the left and a chevron-right at the right indicating the whole card is the tap target → Exercise & Activity tab landing.
Stat payload
Four numbers per spec: Workouts logged (n / n planned), Active minutes (n / 300 target), Calories burned (kcal), Steps. Active-minutes carries a 4pt fill bar against the target; the rest are bare numbers with caption sub-labels.
Week bar strip
Seven mini bars of active minutes Mon–Sun. Today in color.primary.orange #E87A1D, past days in color.text.muted, future days in color.bg.border (the "empty track" tone, so future days don't shout). Day-letter caption beneath each bar.
Variations
V1 2×2 stat grid + week strip — the spec primary. V2 single hero number (active minutes) with sparkline and 3 sub-stats chipped underneath — read-at-a-glance posture. V3 Apple-Fitness-style move ring + vertical stat list + mini week strip at the bottom edge — most visually distinct, treats minutes-toward-target as a closing ring.
Empty state
Single panel inside the card with a "figure-walk" glyph, the coach copy No movement logged yet today., and two CTAs: Start workout (primary) and Connect Apple Health (secondary). Keeps the card height so the dashboard layout doesn't reflow.
