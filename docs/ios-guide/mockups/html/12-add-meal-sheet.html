Mockup notes · Step 4

Sheet
86% screen height, radius.lg 28pt top corners, grabber centered on top. Cancel-X left, "Add a meal" title centered, "Save" right (disabled gray until something is captured). Slides up over a dimmed Log tab; tab bar peeks through at the bottom edge for orientation.
Segmented control
Three options: Photo / Search / Scan, in that order. Inactive cells transparent on the bg.borderSoft track; active cell flips to bg.elevated #0F1117 with white 600 text — the same control vocabulary as M04's height units.
Photo mode · V1
Full-bleed camera viewfinder inset 16pt with radius.lg rounding. Top-left/right SF Pro reticles, a glassy "Auto-detect on" capsule centered up top with a pulse dot, and a coach hint banner ("Point at your plate — I'll parse it") that auto-dismisses on capture. Shutter at the bottom: 68pt circular shutter flanked by the photo library tile (left) and flash toggle (right). Captured photos route to 3.13 Food Detail with the parsed items pre-filled.
Search mode · V2
Search field with a leading magnifier and trailing voice glyph. Caret active on render. Results section card shows highlighted query matches: "grilled chicken bowl" etc. Each row has a 40pt tinted thumbnail, name with the matched substring highlighted yellow, source meta (USDA / Brand / Recipe / Restaurant), trailing kcal stack and a 30pt + button to add. The recents row sits beneath the results for one-tap re-logging.
Scan mode · V3
Dark viewfinder same dimensions as Photo, but with a green-brand corner-bracket reticle around a centered 260×150 zone. A faint horizontal scan-line glows through the box. On match, a found-product card descends from the top with the brand glyph, name, brand · serving size, and a green CONNECTED-style pill showing the kcal estimate; below is a "Save 1 serving" CTA. Below the camera, a status helper at the bottom: "Align barcode within the frame · auto-captures."
Recents
Horizontal chip row with 30pt circular thumbs in food-category gradients. Each chip carries the food name + a divided kcal sublabel. Tap the chip to log a single serving of that food at the same time as the photo path — primary "re-log" affordance.
Save bar
Pinned at sheet bottom on Search & Scan modes once a result is present. Two buttons: ghost "More options" → 3.13 portion editor, gradient.brand "Save to Log" → commits and dismisses the sheet.
