Mockup notes · M29 widget studies

Eyebrow & time window
ENERGY BALANCE · TODAY in body 700 caption, secondary color. Below it a 4-segment pill row [Today][Week][Month][Year] in bg.base with the active selection in text.primary (inverted chip). Window switches scoped to the widget — full-screen view (M30) inherits this state on tap.
Outer ring · ingested
16pt stroke. Three macro arcs in canon colors: protein #00D278, carbs #F59E0B, fat #A855F7. Round line-caps render the tiny end-dots automatically. A faint rgba(15,17,23,0.04) track underneath holds the ring shape when arcs don't fill the circle.
Inner ring · expended
10pt stroke (lighter weight to subordinate to intake). Three source arcs: BMR #6B7280, activity #00B4D8, workout #E87A1D. BMR is always the largest band by definition — it sets the eye's baseline for "how much you'll burn just existing".
Center
Massive DM Sans 700 net number with a signed prefix (+128, −320) at 44pt (V1), 40pt (V2 compact), 54pt (V3 dense). Below: kcal unit caption. Arrow chip: success-tinted circle with arrow.down.right for deficit, warning-tinted circle with arrow.up.right for surplus.
Balance pill
Left-aligned beneath the ring. In range at success-10% / success-text, Slight surplus at warning, Over by X kcal at error. Border at 32% alpha, text at full saturation.
Legend strip
V1 stacks two rows (In / Out) of three swatch+value chips. V2 swaps the inner ring for a 12pt segmented mini-bar (expenditure summary in fewer pixels). V3 pulls the legend to the right of the ring, two-column.
Empty state
Ring collapses to a single dashed track. Center renders 0 kcal logged today in muted disabled color, with a primary + Log first meal CTA. Tapping the card opens the same M30 destination — empty state there.
