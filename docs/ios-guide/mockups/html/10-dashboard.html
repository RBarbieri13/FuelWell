Mockup notes · Step 4

Verdict card · top of content
The decision-engine surface, per DESIGN.md § Components → ScreenScaffold rule: verdict is always above the data. Uses the inverted color.bg.elevated #0F1117 highlight row as the canonical workhorse for emphasis in a light theme. Title in Outfit 600 22pt with a gradient-text accent on the operative phrase; subtitle reads the supporting context in color.text.onDarkMuted. Primary CTA inside is gradient.brand with elevation.cta; secondary CTA is the ghost "Why?" link. Verdict pulse-dot color tracks the verdict tier — success-green on track, warning-amber on caution.
Calories card
White card, radius.lg, elevation.1. Hero number in DM Sans 600 32pt tabular followed by the target in muted color; a small chip on the right shows the running delta (+/− vs. plan) in color.semantic.success tint when in budget, warning tint when projected over. 32pt 7-bar histogram below shows the week's calorie trend — today is the success-green bar, past days are color.text.primary, future is the dim track.
Macros band
The dark inverted-row from DESIGN.md — color.bg.elevated #0F1117, radius.lg. Three columns separated by hairline dividers in color.bg.border.dark. Each column carries an 8pt macro-token swatch, label in color.text.onDarkMuted, value in DM Sans 600 22pt, "of N" beneath, and a 4pt fill bar in the macro color. In V2 the fat bar overflows to color.semantic.error red to telegraph the over-limit state — same shape, different color, no text change.
Week schedule
Seven-day strip, each tile radius.md with the weekday letter, date number in DM Sans 600, and a verdict-color dot beneath. Today is the inverted dark tile so the day reads as the focus point of every glance. Future days drop to color.bg.base with disabled text — the engine has nothing to say there yet.
Workout + Habit panes
Side-by-side cards in a 2-column row. Workout pane shows the next session ("Strength · Push, 5pm") with three numeric chips (45 min · 4 lifts · 90% comp). Habit pane shows a 7×N dot calendar in color.semantic.premium purple — purple is the habit tracking accent color from DESIGN.md (deliberately distinct from macro / verdict colors so the eye can route by hue alone).
Coach nudge
3pt premium-purple left accent (the FuelWell coach's signature stripe). Avatar uses the same gradient as the brand mark. Body is one or two sentences in coach voice referencing today's real data — never generic. Quick-reply chips beneath open the Coach tab pre-loaded with the response. Only renders when the engine has a trigger — never as a placeholder.
Meals list · Today
Compact rows: 36pt color-coded meal-type icon, name + meta line (time, calorie subtotal, source: recipe or restaurant), trailing kcal number. Pending slots ("Dinner") render with a dashed border and the coach's suggested kcal in muted text — a soft commitment the user can replace with a tap. Header carries a live total + the macro fingerprint of what's already eaten.
FAB
56pt circular Quick-Add fab, gradient.brand + a strong shadow. Floats 22pt above the tab bar at the right edge. Opens 3.12 Add Meal sheet. Same placement on every variation — anchor for muscle memory.
Tab bar
iOS-style five-tab bar with blur surface. Tabs: Home (active), Log, Coach, Learn, Progress. Active state is filled glyph + color.text.primary; inactive is line glyph at color.text.muted. 30pt bottom inset for the home-indicator gap.
