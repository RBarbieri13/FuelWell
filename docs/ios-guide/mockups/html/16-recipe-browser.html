Mockup notes · Step 4

Top segmented
Three options: For your macros (default) · Browse all · Saved. Active cell uses bg.elevated. The "For your macros" tab carries a small green count badge showing how many fits the engine found ("12").
Remaining-macros banner
Inverted dark band with the "WHAT'S LEFT" eyebrow, a 20pt headline ("714 kcal, lean protein needed") with a gradient-text accent on the operative phrase, and the three macro fingerprints beneath. Same vocabulary as M14's strap.
Top-3 carousel · V1
Three larger cards horizontally — recipe photo with a glassy fit-percentage badge top-left (gradient-brand on top pick, dark glass on the others) and a heart save-button top-right. Card body carries the name (Outfit/Inter 700 14pt, 2-line clamp), time · servings meta, and a macro fingerprint with the protein number bumped.
"More fits" grid
2-column grid beneath the carousel — same recipe-card vocabulary but trimmed: 1:0.78 image, name, time meta, footer with kcal stack and a green fit-mini chip showing the kcal-budget delta. Cards carry an optional left-corner protein tag for recipes that close the protein gap specifically.
Browse-all · V2
Banner collapses; search field appears up top. Filter chip row goes wide — All / Hi-protein / Lower carb / 30-min / Vegetarian / Pantry-only. First chip stays selected. Grid drops the fit-mini chip and the engine ranking; results sort by relevance to filter set.
Saved · V3
Three collection rows up top (stacked 64pt thumb stacks): Weeknight wins / Hi-protein staples / Sam-approved. Below: 2-col grid of individually-saved recipes with the heart filled. Search reduces scope to saved-only.
FAB
"+" FAB in the bottom-right opens the "Add custom recipe" sheet (out of scope for Pilot, but the entry exists from day 1).
