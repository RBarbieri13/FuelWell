Mockup notes · Step 4

Hero · V1
16:9 photo with the category pill ("MACROS") in the top-left. Floating glass back / share / heart-saved nav overlays the hero — they collapse into the frosted compact nav as the user scrolls.
Title + meta
Outfit 700 24pt title, 1.18 line-height. Meta row: read time (DM Sans), category pill, publish date · author. Reading-progress hairline lives just under the status bar and fills gradient.brand as the user scrolls.
Body type
V1/V2 use Inter 400 15.5pt at 1.62 line-height — the smart-friend body voice. The lead paragraph bumps to 17pt 500 to set the tone. V3 switches to Lora 400 17pt 1.65 with italic h2s — the immersive Reading mode.
Pull quote + number callout
Pull quote: 3pt success-green left accent on a 5% green tint, with one phrase getting the green→blue gradient-text accent. Number callout: white card with the standout figure in DM Sans 700 40pt + a one-sentence explanation — used once per article max to surface "the number" the article is built around.
Inline image
4:3 ratio, 16pt radius. One per article max per spec. Captions sit as small glass pills inside the image.
Takeaway card · core moment
Dark elevated card, "ONE THING TO TRY TODAY" eyebrow with the brand-green pulse, the action statement in Outfit 600 19pt with the operative phrase in gradient-text. Primary "Try it · log a 30g lunch" gradient-brand CTA + ghost "Skip for now" button. The single mandatory element of every article.
Sticky takeaway · V2
Mid-scroll, the dark takeaway pins above the tab bar in a compact form — label + action + small gradient pill CTA — so the "one thing to try" is never out of reach. Returns to inline-card form once the user reaches the end of the article.
Reading mode · V3
No hero; an opaque sticky title bar replaces it. Body type swaps to Lora, drop-cap on the lead paragraph. Takeaway breaks out of the narrow column slightly with a wider full-width card — designed to read as a beat in the article rather than a CTA add-on.
