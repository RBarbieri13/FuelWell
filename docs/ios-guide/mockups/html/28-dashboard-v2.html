Top bar v2
Three-up: hamburger menu (left) opens the full nav drawer (Habit Tracking lives here now), compact FuelWell wordmark center with a today-stamp, help icon (right). No avatar in the bar — avatar moves into the menu drawer.
Tier 1 · Health Score hero
White card, elev.2, radius.lg. Eyebrow HEALTH SCORE. Massive DM Sans 700 score at 60pt (e.g. 86.4). Right-side composite breakdown stacks the five components in DESIGN order: Nutrition 35%, Training 25%, Sleep 20%, Recovery 10%, Body comp 10% — each as a percentage fill so the user can see at a glance which pillar is dragging the average. Delta pill below the score (▲ 4.2 last 30d) and a Why? link in color.semantic.info to the explainer sheet (M26). Day-1 fallback swaps the score for a 92pt dashed placeholder ring and the coach copy: Building your baseline — 7 days of data unlocks your Health Score.
Tier 1 · Inflows/Outflows dual ring
Centerpiece, white card, radius.lg. 200pt SVG with two concentric rings. Outer ring = calories ingested, segmented by macro (protein #00D278 · carbs #F59E0B · fat #A855F7). Inner ring = calories expended, segmented by source (BMR #6B7280 · activity #00B4D8 · workout #E87A1D). Centered: net kcal in DM Sans 700 30pt with a directional arrow chip (success-green for deficit, warning-amber for surplus). Balance pill beneath: In range (success), Slight surplus (warning), Over by X kcal (error). Tap "Expand →" → opens M30 Sankey full-screen view. Empty state replaces the center with a tappable + circle and the copy Log your first meal.
Tier 1 · Verdict CTA
The decision-engine surface, identical pattern from M10 but compressed (19pt headline vs 22pt) since two visual blocks sit above it now. Inverted color.bg.elevated surface with verdict-tier radial wash and pulsing eyebrow dot.
FOLD marker
Dashed full-width hairline at the 852pt mark — the actual viewport bottom. Everything above is Tier 1 (must-see, never below). Everything below requires scroll.
Tier 2 · row of two + DWM
Activity overview (steps + small step-bars sparkline + active minutes), Nutrition overview (kcal logged + 3-row macro micro-bars). Below them, a "My day / week / month" segmented control feeding a 7-bar stacked chart of intake vs expenditure across the selected window.
Tier 3 · progressive disclosure
Progress overview (one-line trend + 110pt weight sparkline) — always visible. Daily Recap (COACH RECAP · 8 PM) — visible only after 8pm; Variation 2 shows it active, Variation 1 hides it. Coach nudge — event-driven; absent in V1, present in V2 (caution swap suggestion). All three slots collapse to nothing on Day-1.
