Mockup notes · M32 Exercise tab landing

Header
Display 700 26pt title "Exercise & Activity" with a small day-stamp sub-line ("Fri · 5 days this week"). Trailing 36pt + button uses gradient.brand for the primary quick-add. No back chevron — tab roots never have one.
Today's workout hero
Inverted bg.elevated surface with an orange radial wash (the training accent), display 700 22pt workout name, three info chips (duration, lifts, projected kcal), and an orange "Start workout" CTA. Rest-day flips to a light surface; pulse dot drops to text.disabled; CTA becomes ghost "Add a workout".
This week strip
Horizontal scroll of 7 chips. Today reads as the inverted dark chip with the orange status pill ("Workout"); other days carry a light pill colored by their status (workout · rest · upcoming). Minutes line beneath. The strip wraps Mon–Sun and is sticky-scrollable horizontally.
Quick access
Six destinations per spec: Workout Log, Activity Tracker, Workout Plans, Exercise Library, Schedule, Trainer workouts. V1 & V3 render as a single grouped list (iOS-pattern). V2 renders as a 2-column card grid for visual scan. Icons follow semantic tones — orange for workouts/plans, info-blue for tracker, premium-purple for library, success-green for schedule, warning-amber for trainer.
Recent workouts
Last 3 logged sessions. V1 compact rows (name · date · duration + kcal trailing). V2 same row pattern (rows are easier to scan in two-pane layouts). V3 a single horizontally-scrolling cards row, swipeable.
Empty state (first-time)
Hero swaps to a coach-voice prompt: No workouts logged yet. Want a quick suggestion from coach, or pick a plan? Two CTAs side-by-side: gradient.brand Coach suggest and ghost Browse plans. This-week strip still renders (chips read "rest" / "upcoming"). Recent-workouts section collapses to a dashed coach-voice placeholder.
