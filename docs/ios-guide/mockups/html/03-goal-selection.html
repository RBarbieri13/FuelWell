Mockup notes · Step 4

Progress dots
Nine 7pt dots centered in the 44pt nav row. Three filled color.text.primary #0A0A0F, six empty color.text.disabled #C7CCD3. Back chevron sits left; right cell is a spacer so dots remain optically centered to the device.
Title block
H1 in Outfit 700 28pt -0.022em — text.title.lg. Subtitle in Inter 400 16pt color.text.secondary. Real screen copy: "What's your goal?" / "We'll tune your plan around it." — no banned vocabulary.
Goal card · default
White surface, radius.lg 20pt, 1pt color.bg.border hairline, elevation.1. 16pt internal padding. Stacked with 12pt gaps. Cards are 16pt off the screen edges.
Goal card · selected
Border switches to 1.5pt color.semantic.success #00D278. Fill becomes the rgba(0,210,120,.05) on-track tint — close to the calorie-ring vocabulary the user will meet on the dashboard. Radio fills with the same green.
Icon containers
40 × 40pt tinted square, 10pt corner. Background uses the icon's semantic color at 10% alpha; the SF Symbol stroke takes the full-saturation hue. Original FuelWell artwork drawn to SF-Symbol-equivalent geometry — not lifted from the system library.
fat/premium · #A855F7 @ 10% info · #00B4D8 @ 10% success · #00D278 @ 10%
Goal copy
Real app copy, not wireframe placeholders. Lose weight — "Calorie deficit with a high-protein floor." Maintain weight — "Hold your current weight, lock in habits." Gain weight — "Lean surplus with progressive intake." One line each; text.body.sm 14pt secondary color.
Helper · premium gate
text.caption 12pt color.text.muted. A small lock glyph in color.semantic.premium #A855F7 sits before the line — the only chrome that hints at Premium without turning into an upsell. No modal, no chip, no badge.
Continue · two states
Disabled (V1): dark secondary fill (color.text.primary @ 0.42 opacity) — affordance present but inert. Enabled (V2/V3): gradient.brand with the elevation.cta green halo. Same 48pt height, radius.md 12pt, Inter 600 17pt — same position in the dock.
Sticky CTA dock
Anchored to the bottom 34pt above the home indicator. A 2pt top fade to bg.base prevents the cards from butting flush into the button when content extends. CTA never scrolls.
