Mockup notes · Step 4

Progress dots
Eight of nine filled. Final onboarding step before plan reveal (3.9).
Hero
92pt rounded square (radius.xl) on white with two faint color.semantic.info pulse rings to telegraph "this is the notification screen." Bell glyph in info-blue at full saturation — neutral, non-celebratory color per DESIGN.md "Anti-patterns".
Title block
"Let your coach reach you." in Outfit 700 26pt, centered. Subtitle: "Event-driven only. We send when something on your plan changes — never on a schedule, never to upsell."
Quiet-hours promise pill
Centered below the subtitle, a single pill on white with a moon glyph and the time range "10:00 PM – 7:00 AM". Hard-coded at Pilot per the wireframe spec — surfaced as a promise, not a setting. Settings exposes it read-only.
Trigger rows
Four-row card on white, radius.lg, with the same anatomy as M07's metric rows. Each row carries a tinted SF-Symbol icon, a trigger name in Inter 600 15pt, and a one-line consequence in color.text.secondary: meal skipped → warning; macros over → error; missed workout → info-blue; weight off plan → purple. Colors are tonal, not categorical — they hint at the kind of nudge without being a verdict.
CTA — always enabled
"Enable Notifications" with the bell glyph. Permission screens never disable the primary action; the iOS alert on tap is the one shot we get. Skip-for-now in color.text.secondary beneath — advances to 3.9 without firing the system prompt; user can recover later in Settings → Notifications (capped by the OS-level setting).
iOS system alert · V2
Native iOS alert dialog — 270pt-wide rounded card with 92% white blur, "FuelWell" Would Like to Send You Notifications" in SF 17 semibold, subtitle "Notifications may include alerts, sounds, and icon badges. These can be configured in Settings." in SF 13. Two-column buttons — "Don't Allow" / "Allow" — bottom-right is the default per Apple HIG so we render it in 600.
Lock-screen preview · V3
After tap-Allow, the device transitions to a dark gradient lock-screen mock with the iOS native clock (Sunday · 12:38) and two stacked notification banners — the first is the real "You skipped lunch" coach push, the second is a softened second-priority example. iOS-native rounded blur-bg, 0.5pt white border, the FuelWell gradient app glyph at 22pt. Bottom of frame fades to a "Notifications enabled — continue" label + dark Continue CTA so the flow keeps moving.
Verdict
No verdict surface yet — engine still has no plan. Reveal is 3.9.
