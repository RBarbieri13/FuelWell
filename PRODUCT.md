# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FuelWell has two primary surfaces. The iOS app is for Founding 100 users who want a calm nutrition, activity, and coaching companion that tells them what to do next without shaming them or turning every meal into a spreadsheet. The build dashboard is for Robert, Max, and development agents who need an honest operating picture of what has shipped, what is waiting for review, what is blocked, and which plan gates are still unproven.

## Product Purpose

FuelWell exists to help users make better daily health decisions from food, activity, recovery, and AI coaching context. The development cockpit exists to keep the build process evidence-based: current plan state, open PRs, gate results, and next work should be visible without relying on chat memory or stale checklist documents.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and premium. FuelWell should feel supportive and exacting at the same time: warm enough for health behavior, precise enough for real operations, and restrained enough that users trust it under repeated daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, motivational fitness clutter, neon AI tooling, washed-out low-contrast text, decorative gradients, nested card grids, and vague progress language that makes partial scaffolding feel complete. Operational surfaces must not hide blockers behind optimistic percentages.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence beats optimism. A status is only complete when a gate or artifact proves it.
2. One next action. Every screen should make the current decision or blocker obvious.
3. Premium restraint. Use strong hierarchy, readable text, and purposeful color instead of decoration.
4. No dead affordances. Buttons, chevrons, and cards either do something real or clearly state why they are unavailable.
5. Design for repeated use. Dense information is acceptable when it helps Robert, Max, or a pilot user make a faster decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast at minimum. Body copy should be readable at normal mobile and desktop sizes, motion should respect reduced-motion preferences, and state must not be color-only. Health copy must avoid shame, moralizing, or panic language.
