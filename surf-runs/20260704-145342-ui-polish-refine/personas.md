# FuelWell surf run — persona roster (80 personas, 20 sets of 4)

Derived from Phase 1 answer to "who actually uses this project, and for what?":
FuelWell is a consumer AI nutrition + fitness coaching app (web app with sample-data
preview mode; iOS sibling). Real users and jobs-to-be-done:

1. **Log meals fast** (search / photo / barcode; "under 10 seconds" is a stated product goal)
2. **Ask the AI coach real-world food questions** and get personalized, allergy-aware answers
3. **Check today's macros/calories on the dashboard** and course-correct
4. **Plan food**: meal plan, recipes, grocery list
5. **Track training**: workouts, activity, recovery
6. **Review progress**: weight trend, daily review, streaks
7. **Set up & maintain identity**: onboarding wizard, profile, settings
8. **Evaluate the product**: founders/testers/investors walking the /preview hub before launch

Primary audience: "Intentional Beginners" — 25–38 working professionals, tried and
abandoned 2–4 nutrition apps. Secondary: dietary-restricted (vegan/keto/allergy), 40–55
metabolic-change users, adaptive athletes, postpartum users. Mostly phone-first, time-poor,
allergic to judgmental or spreadsheet-like UX.
